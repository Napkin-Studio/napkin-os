LUMEN WATER BRAND BOOK (excerpt)

p.6  Positioning
Lumen Water is the water that tastes of where it comes from: clear, cold and unhurried.

p.9  Voice
We sound clear, bright, unhurried and warm. We say "water", never "hydration".
Say it plainly. Say it once.

p.14 The mark
The Lumen mark is a single droplet over the word Lumen. Keep clear around it
the height of the droplet, on every side. Never stretch it.

p.15 Colour
Lumen blue #0E5FA8 carries the brand. Spring white #F7FAFC is the ground.
Stone #5B6770 for support. Ink #0B1F2E for text. Sun #F2B134 as a spark, never a ground.
Never use red.

p.18 Type
Headlines are set in Lumen Display. Body copy is set in DM Sans.
