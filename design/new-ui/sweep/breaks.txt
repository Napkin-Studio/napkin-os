**Breakage sweep: napkin-app-v1.html**

I clicked through every screen and state on the list, at 1600×1000 and 1280×800, in light and dark, for brands wk and tbwa. No page errors appeared in any run. All screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-1000/napkin-shots/sweep/` (paths below are relative to that folder).

### P0: broken function or hidden feature

1. **Brief output overflows at 1280.**
   - Steps: `?tool=brief&step=output`, viewport 1280×800.
   - What's wrong: `#brief-workspace` is a fixed `860px 440px` grid plus gap, which is wider than 1280. The page starts at x=-26 and the side tab touches the edge. The inspector is cut off on the right ("edited 2 days ag…", buttons clipped) and the page scrolls sideways (scrollWidth 1306).
   - Shot: `m-1280-wk-light-brief-output.png`, `m-1280-tbwa-dark-brief-output.png`
   - Fix: `#brief-workspace{grid-template-columns:minmax(0,860px) minmax(320px,440px)}`, plus a `@media (max-width:1360px)` step that narrows the inspector to 360px.

2. **"Ask the research" shows no answer.**
   - Steps: type a question, click Ask.
   - What's wrong: the toast says "Answered with 2 cited pins", but the answer is written into `.r-ans-q`, which is hidden (it sits in the "answer formed" card the owner removed). Nothing visible changes.
   - Shot: `r-asked.png`
   - Fix: render the question and answer as a thread under the Ask box in `#r-side`.

3. **Research "Lock and accept" does nothing.**
   - Steps: (a) click it while one call is open: no toast, nothing happens. (b) Click Keep first, then Lock: the chip stays "Draft".
   - Shot: `r-locked2.png`
   - Fix: in the `#r-lock` handler, show a toast while calls are open (the brief already does this: "1 part needs your call…"). Otherwise set `#r-rep-chip` to Locked, swap the button to Unlock, and set the status pill.

4. **Keep and Cut don't update the report.**
   - Steps: click Keep, or click Cut.
   - What's wrong: the stats bar updates. But the sentence pills still say "needs you" (3 of them), "Confirm before the brief" still lists Finding 3, and after Cut summary line 5 and the Finding 3 sentence are still there.
   - Shot: `r-kept.png`, `r-cut.png`
   - Fix: re-render `#r-answer`, `.prov-pill.needs` and `#rb-cont` from the call state.

5. **Decisions drawer "Approve both & lock brief" doesn't reach the brief.**
   - What's wrong: after approving, the brief is still DRAFT and its panel still says "Your call · 2 open". The Decisions badge shows 1 instead of 0, and the status pill reads "DONE · VERIFIED".
   - Shot: `decisions-approved.png`
   - Fix: send the approval through the same state function the brief's Yes/No buttons use, then run the lock.

6. **Brief inspector slides under the top bar when you scroll.**
   - What's wrong: `#look-inspector` is `position:sticky; top:20px`, but the top bar is about 63px tall. Save·PDF is cut off, and the source card's "← back" link and red dot are hidden.
   - Shot: `brief-srccard.png`, `brief-resized.png`
   - Fix: `#look-inspector{top:calc(var(--tb-h,63px) + 16px);max-height:calc(100vh - var(--tb-h,63px) - 32px)}`

7. **"Needs your call" is hidden whenever the agent status shows.**
   - What's wrong: both share `#bar-status`. On the desk, the client page and the prompt screens it reads "SLEEPING · IDLE" even though McDonald's has 2 calls waiting. It also disappears during runs and redrafts.
   - Shot: `p-desk-home.png`, `brief-redraft-after.png`
   - Fix: make the needs pill its own element that always shows when the count is above 0, and give the agent status a separate chip.

8. **Client page rows open the wrong thing.**
   - "Spoon trial" (a Draft brief) opens the "New brief" prompt.
   - "Delivery desserts" (research, agents working) shows the Irish summer treats run: same question, same eyebrow.
   - Decks "+" silently opens the existing Q3 deck.
   - "38 brand facts · 112 category facts · 61 decisions" is a dead link.
   - Shot: `client-delivery-desserts.png`
   - Fix: give each `.cp-row` its own doc id and state, and make Decks "+" create a new deck.

9. **A newly added client doesn't work.**
   - Clicking the Tayto circle opens no dial.
   - The eyebrow stays at "4 clients" after adding a fifth.
   - Submitting the form empty does nothing and shows no message.
   - Shot: `added-client.png`, `added-client-dial.png`
   - Fix: wire the dial for new `.cl-circle` elements, recount `#hero-eyebrow` after adding, and validate `#cl-add-name`.

10. **A locked brief can still be edited.**
    - What's wrong: after "Brief locked", double-click still makes the part editable, and Edit, Redraft… and Move up/down are still shown. It also says "Locked by Siobhán Ryan", but she is the client contact named in the call notes.
    - Fix: check the lock state in the dblclick, `sp-edit`, redraft and move handlers, and disable those buttons while locked.

11. **"Back to prompt" and "Open finished brief" never appear.**
