The Deep O logo

Deep O is Napkin Studio's one mark: a sphere with a window, a sky over a sea whose horizon is the agency's accent, and a ring that shows progress. One engine draws it everywhere. Its geometry is in app/src/brand/deepOSvg.ts, its state grammar in deepO.ts (a port of design/new-ui/identity-v4/mark/deep-o-grammar.js), and the component is DeepOMark.tsx. Nothing else draws the mark.

States

The marks below are the static cut (staticMarkSvg) rendered from the real code at 96px (the satin cut, over 24px) and 20px (the small cut, 24px and under), in Napkin's colours. The host owns the state, and the mark only eases toward it. It never changes state by itself.

Two cuts

Over 24px: satin (H1). Sphere gradient from the base, window, sky and sea, horizon wave in the accent, progress ring r=44.2 with a halo. Used for the bar medallion (64px), sign-in, the boot mark (64px), and loading signs.
24px and under: small cut. Flat tones, a smaller and deeper window, and no horizon line (at that size it read as a minus sign or a Pokéball). A mark too close to its surface (below 1.6:1) gets a rim in ink or paper (rimFor). Used for the favicon (16px) and inline loading signs (20px).

When each state happens in the studio

  • The bar's mark is shared: one model for the whole studio (shared in components/markStore.ts), so leaving the desk keeps its state. Other marks each have their own model.
  • Needs is local. The desk counts calls across all clients, but the medallion changes to needs only when the app you are in needs attention (owner decision, new-ui-shell).
  • Follow the mouse while awake. The shared mark leans toward the pointer (pointerTarget, stepLook) only while its state is awake. It is off under reduced motion and in every other state.
  • Sleep after 30 s. IDLE_MS = 30000 in markStore.ts (owner, 2026-11-11). Any movement, scroll or key wakes it. A hidden tab sleeps at once. Before the first engagement it is already asleep.

Boot and late show

Loading signs appear only on a slow wait (brand/lateShow.ts). A sign waits 400ms, then fades in over 300ms. A sign that mounts within 80ms of the last one carries on that wait. The boot screen, then the sign-in check, then "Opening the studio…" all keep one clock, so the mark never vanishes and fades in again on the way to the desk. Late signs use a negative animation-delay (lateDelay) to start partway through the fade.

Reduced motion

Under reduced motion there is no breath, no wave, no turning arc and no pointer follow, and frames are drawn only while the mark settles. An indeterminate wait breathes its arc (opacity .55–1 over 2.4 s) so there is still a sign of life (DEEP_O_CSS @media (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce)).

Do

Use <DeepOMark state=… progress=…/>, or staticMarkSvg for a page with no React. Pass real progress (steps done / steps) or none.

Don't

Draw the mark yourself, fake progress, or show a spinner of your own. Do not use the mark as an agent: agents are spheres.
