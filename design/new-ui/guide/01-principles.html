01 · The eight rules every screen follows

Principles

Agents write most of this code, so these rules come first. Each rule names where it is enforced in the build, so you can check your screen against a real one. When a rule and a screen disagree, the rule wins and the screen is a bug.

One thing at a time

A panel shows either the whole document or one selected item. It never stacks one on the other. Selecting a part replaces the brief view, and Esc, the crumb or a click on empty paper bring it back. There are two levels at most below the crumb.

Source: /tmp/…/sidetab/final.md "State (a)/(b)", rsidebar/final.md §1. Built in brief-maker/index.html (#briefView / #selPart) and campaign-research/index.html (#pv).

Paper sheets

Documents sit on paper: a warm sheet (--paper, #FFFDF8 before the agency tint) with --shadow-paper, on a desk (--desk). The sheet is what prints. Brief Maker's PDF is the same 12-column sheet at the same page breaks. On paper there are no glyphs, no agent spheres and no coloured state chips, apart from the amber "Needs your call" tag on a part.

No floating drawers

History, lists, facts and sources open inside the side panel, under its crumb. The only overlay that slides in is the Decisions drawer (540px, from the bar). A tool's own detail never opens a drawer over the page.

The panel never repeats the page

The panel names a thing and lights it on the paper. It never quotes it. A part is named by its heading ("The job"), a list line by "line 3 of 7", and a finding by its number and section ("◗1 §1 The market"). Text that matches the sheet is hidden from Built from.

Do

"← Brief › Where we are" in the crumb, and the part lit on the sheet.

Don't

A "This line" box that quotes the sentence the person just clicked.

One primary action per view

Each view has one filled button: Lock the brief in the brief view, Edit (then Done) in a part view, Lock the report, Keep, Confirm or Open in Research. Everything else is .sp-btn (outlined, 28px) or .side-link (an underlined word). The primary fill is --ag-act, the agency's base in light and its accent in dark.

Do

One btn-rect primary per view, the rest outlined.

Don't

Save, Draft again and Lock all filled side by side (the old tab had three).

Plain words

Sentence case. No ids, keys, codes, contract names, "pins", "lens" or persona names on the surface. Agents are named by role ("the Review agent"). See Copy rules.

Every line is clickable

Every row in a panel opens something and ends in ›. Every sentence in a report is a clan-field you can select. Chips are attributes and never act as buttons. Hovering shows a short tooltip at most. A click always goes to the panel.

Agents appear in one place only

Working agents appear on the run stage (the large sphere) and, while you are in a document, as one 44px job line at the top of the side panel (.side-agent). They never appear in the top bar. The bar shows the job as words ("Working · step N/M") and the Deep O mark changes state. See Agents.
