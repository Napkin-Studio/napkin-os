03 · Nine agencies, one registry, derived tokens

Agency looks

An agency's look (its "template") comes from one registry: AGENCY_COLOURS in app/src/shell/agency/palette.ts. An admin sets a display name, a darker base and an accent. Every other token, in light and dark, is derived by paletteOf, pillOf, surfaceCss and markOf. Nobody picks their own colours (owner, 9 Nov). The swatches below come from running the real palette.ts functions.

On the live desk

Sign-in: the look switches as you type

agencyOfSignInName reads only the registry, never the roster or the server, so the look says nothing about who has an account. The card takes the look as soon as the text reads @<id>, an alias (bg, boysgirls, b+g) or an email at <id>.<tld> (x@tbwa.co.uk). With no @ yet, or an unknown agency, the card stays neutral: warm graphite #4A453E on #D9D1C3, never Napkin's blue.

empty → neutral marktype “x@tb”still neutraltype “x@tbwa”TBWA look: mark, field ring, button, desk tint

The agencies with a wordmark (boysandgirls, catapult, pluto) show it on the card above the title. The others show the Deep O in their colours and their name in the line "Sign in to <name> on Napkin Studio."

The boot look

Before any module runs, index.html paints the boot screen in the look of the agency this browser last signed in to (localStorage napkin.lastAgency, theme from napkin.theme). It is generated from palette.ts by brand/bootLook.ts (written into the page by vite.config.ts deepOBoot). The mark is a 64px static, CSS-turned "working" Deep O. It and its words appear only after 400ms and fade in over 300ms, so a quick load shows nothing but the right background. A browser that has never signed in boots in the neutral graphite mark on DESK_BASE (#F6F1E7 / #121110).

How an admin adds an agency

  1. Add one line to AGENCY_COLOURS in app/src/shell/agency/palette.ts, keyed by the lower-case id after the @: 
    harbour: { name: 'Harbour & Co', base: '#0F6B5C', accent: '#9FE6D8' },
  2. Optional: put the logo files through scripts/agency-logos.mjs, which sanitises them into logos.ts. Then set logo (wordmark for a light ground), logoOnDark and icon (square). Without them, the name and initials stand in.
  3. Optional: pill, accentUse, surface, accentInk (above). Other spellings go in AGENCY_ALIASES.
  4. tests/agencyPalette.test.ts and tests/agencySkins.test.tsx check that the new look reads at 4.5:1. The change is reviewed like a roster change. Branding says nothing about who has an account.
Open question (new-ui-shell): should admins set colours in the running studio instead of in code, and who is an admin?
