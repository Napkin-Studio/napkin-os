Copy rules

Napkin's people are planners and strategists, not engineers. Every word on the surface is plain, short and in sentence case. These rules come from the owner's decisions in the feature records and the glossary in rsidebar/final.md.

Rules

  1. Plain words. Say what it is ("Couldn't find", "Left out"), not what the system calls it.
  2. No ids, codes, keys, contract names or "pins". src_…, f_012, roster.categories.primary, process:middleware and clan:bar never appear. They live in History → Full record and in Technical details, with Copy.
  3. Role names, not persona names. "the Review agent", from NapkinRoles.the(). Captions are "REVIEW AGENT". The material ("pearl") is never shown (owner, 10 Nov).
  4. Sentence case everywhere. Mono caps only for eyebrows, labels and counts.
  5. Name, don't quote. "Finding 3 in §2", "line 3 of 7", "The job".
  6. Numbers as small pictures where they can be: dots for calls, bars for confidence, pips for used against found.
  7. One word for one thing. "lock" only for "Lock the brief" and "Lock the report". "Keep as is" for a protected part.
  8. Client review never names "Client" as an actor in the decisions log. Irregular past verbs read correctly ("wrote", "kept").
Do

"Uses 30 of 57 sources · 50 of 196 facts"

"The Review agent found a problem across background, audience and mandatories."

"Settle 6 first"

Don't

"USED/PINNED 50/196 · pri 12 sec 9"

"judge@2 verdict on f_011 (d_031)"

"Jude flagged 6 fields"
