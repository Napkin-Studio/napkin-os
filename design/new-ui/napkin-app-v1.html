    Headline

    McFlurry’s summer is won outside, in unplanned social moments, and lost to the sofa when nothing gives a reason to go out.

    Summary
    • Irish 16–24s don't plan treats; they stretch social moments.
    • 68% of out-of-home treat stops are unplanned and social.
    • Delivery desserts grew 23.4% in a year in the Republic.
    • Indulgence moves home unless the outing gives a reason.

    02 — Findings

    What the evidence says

    1. 16–24s plan food stops around social moments, not meals — the treat is the excuse to stay out.

    Verified
    "Among Irish 16–24s, 68% of out-of-home treat occasions were unplanned and social, up from 61% in 2024."
    Bord Bia Consumer Insights, Jun 2026 · Republic of Ireland sample (n=1,400)
    f_003 · unplanned occasions 68% f_004 · social context +7 pts f_005 · CSO with friends 65%
    DecidedKept as the primary finding grounding the brief's spontaneity insight.
    BecauseTwo independent sources agree within 3 points; the upward trend holds across 2024–2026.
    CertaintyHigh — National representative panel, published methodology.

    2. Frozen desserts through delivery apps grew 23.4% year on year in Ireland — indulgence is moving home.

    Verified
    "Frozen dessert delivery orders in the Republic of Ireland rose 23.4% against the prior 12 months."
    Kantar Worldpanel, Aug 2026 · ROI Out-of-Home & Delivery Tracker
    f_007 · delivery growth +23.4% YoY
    DecidedUsed Kantar's 23.4% figure over the client deck's 31%.
    RejectedClient internal deck (31%) — no stated methodology and likely blends Northern Ireland with ROI.
    CertaintyHigh — Like-for-like Republic of Ireland panel definition.

    3. Two-spoon sharing is already a recognisable McFlurry behaviour on Irish TikTok.

    Proposed · Thin evidence
    "the two spoons thing is so real, nobody orders their own mcflurry"
    TikTok Irish UGC sample, Oct 2026 · 2 clips, 4.1k combined likes
    f_011 · two-spoon UGC (2 clips) f_009 · #McFlurry IE 11.2M views
    DecidedHeld as Proposed only — not auto-verified.
    BecauseThe cultural signal is vivid, but 2 clips is too small a sample to state as a national norm without your sign-off.
    CertaintyLow / Thin — Needs human judgement: keep as creative hook or drop.
