- Consumer Council NI quality weights (AC1 25.2 and so on): unverified search summary, low confidence. Not used.
- Auckland Council 55/45 creative panel: trade-press summary, page returned 403. Not used.
- D&AD three-question order and the LBB quotes ("the idea behind the idea", jury president quote): not found or not verified. Not used.
- Slidebean Airbnb slide counts and the CouchSurfing figure: not on the page. Not used.
- DocSend 3m44s: reported only via TechCrunch 2015; the 2022 article does not contain it. Cited with the 2015 source only.
- Canva "turns a prompt into a personalized, ready-to-edit deck": unverified quote. Not used as a quote.
- Gamma competitor-review claims, PPTEval 0.71 Pearson, Prometheus 0.882/0.392: not verified. Not used.
- IPA eight criteria names and the 3,000-word limit: not confirmed. Not used.
- Zak's oxytocin and donation figures: not verified. Not used.
- The Zheng verbosity figures (91.3%, 8.7%) are cited with a medium-confidence flag: read via a fetch summary.
- Campaign's 4.26 clarity score (ID Comms/4A's): not opened. Not used.

## What the researchers could not find
- Client-side: ANA/4A's ranked selection drivers; the original AAR 25% dataset; original Scopen, Vennli and GO! Network data; ISBA and current WFA guidelines (member-only); any client body's recommended slide limit.
- Tender: Irish OGP guidance itself; a GCS or CCS marketing framework scoring annex; any page limit published by an advertising tender; Irish pitch scoring rubrics with sub-criteria; a norm for quality weighting across tenders.
- Agency structure: Jon Steel's Perfect Pitch and Mark Clark on structure; a verified winning pitch deck with client confirmation; any published scorecard weights.
- Investor: DocSend's original reports (403); any 2024-2026 per-slide times; primary copies of famous decks; any investor scoring rubric; outcome evidence beyond correlational samples.
- Awards: IPA numeric weights and current criteria page; D&AD 2026 kit; primary winning-case write-ups.
- Persuasion: Minto, Duarte, Reynolds primary text; any study that slide count or words per slide predicts a pitch win; peak-end applied to pitches; a procurement study of order or halo effects; Stasser and Titus full text.
- Pitch losses: any study of pitch fatigue or order in agency pitches; UK Cabinet Office common-failure guidance; the TrinityP3 State of the Pitch report itself (2026 figures via Mediaweek only).
- AI evaluation: a judge validated against real pitch outcomes; official Gamma, Canva, Beautiful.ai and Pitch statements on citation; measured hallucination rates for those tools; a study of slide-length bias in judges.
- Repo: no external source for PITCH_MAX=18, the two-minute rule, word budgets or investor slide order; no persuasion metric in evaluate.py; I did not read assemble.py (beyond criteria_coverage), stages.py or prompts, and outputs/ was excluded.
