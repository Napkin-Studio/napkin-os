Findings
- Composition: all three follow every frame.
- Identity: the WAN 3 chain keeps Mira in every shot. P1 keeps her in shots 1-2; in shot 3 her hair goes lighter.
  The Veo chain turns her blonde mid-way through shot 1.
- Landing: WAN 3 and Veo 3.1 Fast both end on a frame visually identical to the requested last frame, so chain joins
  are seamless (first+last on WAN 3 was "untested" in the catalogue; now tight).
- P1 burned the character sheet's swatch strip into shot 1 (the front view carries text and swatches; finding 9).
- Cost: P1 and the WAN 3 chain cost the same for the same length; Veo is about a third more.

Decision (owner's "run both tests, then set the default", 2026-11-16): method A, a chain of clips each starting on
its frame and landing on the next, on WAN 3 by default, joined without a cut.
