The real question




Ferncliff is not launching another canned
coffee; it is giving energy-drink grabbers
a lift they no longer have to excuse.

The fight is not café against can. It is a guilty lift against a
good one, in the same fridge.
BACKGROUND          Ferncliff, a hot roaster that also supplies some cafés, enters cold ready-to-drink
                    coffee in March with a canned Oat Flat White, on a smaller budget than last
                    year's. UK RTD coffee was worth an estimated USD 490.97 million in 2025,
                    forecast to grow 6.08% a year. Costa RTD became an £8m brand within a year.

Insight



People want the lift but not the drink
they have to excuse, so Ferncliff
makes the lift something to enjoy.
People call energy drinks unhealthy, yet keep buying them
for the quick boost. They want the lift but feel they must
excuse the drink. So offer the lift as a small treat they are
glad to be seen with.
Our insight.

57% say the boost is the main reason they buy energy
drinks.

59% think energy drinks are unhealthy.

The tension: desire for energy against health concerns.

Urban commuters and gym-goers aged 25-40, grabbing a

spare and takes whatever is nearest.
