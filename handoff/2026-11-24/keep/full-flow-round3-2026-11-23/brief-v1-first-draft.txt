Proper Crunch
CLIENT
Harrowgate Bakery Co., launching Harrowgate Oven Crisps.
PROJECT
Proper Crunch
BUDGET & SCOPE
Budget is TBC and described as modest; confirm whether it includes VAT and whether media sits inside it. Deliverables: posters near stations and supermarkets, Instagram and TikTok content, a 15-second film for YouTube and cinema, and sampling in meal-deal aisles. Timing: on shelf in March, buzz before Easter, ideas due Thursday. The channel list is wide for a modest budget, so it needs prioritising.
THE JOB
To confirm: Move a meal-deal shopper from reaching for a familiar fried crisp to picking up Harrowgate Oven Crisps, because they believe a baked sourdough crisp can be properly crunchy.
WHERE WE ARE
Harrowgate Bakery Co., known for its bread, is launching Harrowgate Oven Crisps: baked (not fried) sourdough in a 120 g bag. Sea salt & cider vinegar comes first, more flavours later. On shelf in March, with buzz wanted before Easter. It must feel artisan yet sit in supermarket meal deals, where price promotion is the biggest driver of brand choice (43%). Listings are still unconfirmed.
THE BARRIER
In a meal deal, shoppers reach for the crisp they know. They suspect a baked crisp will be a worthy, less satisfying compromise, and an unknown bakery brand has no crisp credentials yet. Only 11% say they buy a 'less guilty' option, so 'healthier' alone will not move them.

OBJECTIVE · COMMERCIAL
Win listings and trial at launch, with meal-deal presence in supermarkets from March and visible buzz before Easter. The client asked for a viral moment and pre-Easter buzz but gave no sales, distribution or trial targets. To confirm.
OBJECTIVE · BEHAVIOURAL
Shoppers choose Harrowgate Oven Crisps in the meal-deal snack slot instead of a big-brand fried crisp, and try the bag. Swapping people from Harrowgate's own sourdough loaves was raised by the client but is a secondary aim to confirm, because it could cannibalise bread.
OBJECTIVE · ATTITUDINAL
People believe a baked sourdough crisp can be properly crunchy, a bit special and still an everyday choice. They feel it is a tasty treat, not a worthy compromise. This keeps the client's wish to feel artisan while living in every meal deal.
WHO WE ARE TALKING TO
Primary: office lunchers and students, mostly 18-35, grabbing a meal deal on a weekday lunchtime and picking the crisp in seconds, often whatever is on promotion. Many snack in place of a proper meal at least weekly (62% of 16-34s). Also food-curious shoppers. Secondary: parents buying for lunchboxes. Not for diet-first shoppers or loyal fried-crisp heavy users. The client said 18-55; the narrower core is our proposal to confirm.
COMPETITOR CONTEXT
Walkers is the benchmark and leans on celebrity-led humour (the Gary Lineker horror spoof). Kettle Chips and Tyrrells hold the 'crafted' space; Pringles the familiar tube; Propercorn the lighter popcorn option. Harrowgate's own bread is a cannibalisation risk. The open space is a baked crisp with real bakery credentials and a crunch it can prove, without fleeing into health cues.
CONSUMER INSIGHT
A meal-deal crisp is the small treat that makes the lunch, and the crunch is the point. People want the satisfaction without the greasy weight afterwards, but they will not trade taste for virtue: taste is the number one purchase driver.
SINGLE-MINDED PROPOSITION
Proper crunch, baked not fried.

THE OBJECTION TO BEAT
To confirm: "Baked crisps are just dull and worthy, and not as good as a proper crisp."
REASONS TO BELIEVE
Baked, not fried.
Made with Harrowgate's 80-year sourdough starter.
No palm oil.
To confirm: '40% less fat than fried crisps' only once the comparator is named. UK rules require the difference to be stated, for the same quantity of food, against a range of same-category foods.
To confirm: 'compostable bag' only once certified. Without a recognised certification mark and product code the bag must be treated as not compostable.
RESPONSE · THINK
To confirm: Baked sourdough crisps can be properly crunchy, and Harrowgate knows what it is doing.
RESPONSE · FEEL
To confirm: Delighted and a little cheeky, as if they have found a better snack that still feels like a treat.
RESPONSE · DO
To confirm: Pick up Harrowgate Oven Crisps in the meal-deal slot instead of a familiar fried bag. Sampling in the aisle and sharing the crunch on social are routes to that pick-up, not separate asks.
