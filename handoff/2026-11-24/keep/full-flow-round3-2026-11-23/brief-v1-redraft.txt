Proper Crunch
CLIENT
Harrowgate Bakery Co., launching Harrowgate Oven Crisps.
PROJECT
Proper Crunch
BUDGET & SCOPE
£180,000 ex VAT, media included. That buys one national poster burst near stations and supermarkets, Instagram and TikTok content, one 15-second film for YouTube and cinema, and sampling in about 200 stores. On shelf in March, buzz before Easter, ideas due Thursday. The pub and bread are out of scope.
THE JOB
To confirm: A meal-deal shopper puts down the familiar fried crisp and picks up Harrowgate Oven Crisps, because they now believe a baked crisp can be properly crunchy.
WHERE WE ARE
Harrowgate Bakery Co., a bread maker, is launching Harrowgate Oven Crisps: baked (not fried) sourdough crisps in a 120 g bag, sea salt & cider vinegar first and more flavours later. They go on shelf in March, with buzz wanted before Easter. Two supermarket meal-deal listings are confirmed and a third is in talks. In this category price promotion drives brand choice for 43% of buyers, and only 11% say they buy a 'less guilty' option. The brand has to feel artisan while living in the meal-deal aisle.
THE BARRIER
In a meal deal, shoppers grab the crisp they know. They suspect a baked crisp is a worthy, less satisfying compromise, and an unknown bakery brand has no crisp credentials yet. Only 11% say they buy a 'less guilty' option, so 'healthier' alone will not move them.
PAGE 2 OF 5
OBJECTIVE · COMMERCIAL
Be listed in 3 of the big 5 supermarket meal deals by March and sell 2 million bags in the first 12 weeks. The after-Easter repeat target is still to be confirmed with the client.
OBJECTIVE · BEHAVIOURAL
Shoppers choose Harrowgate Oven Crisps in the meal-deal snack slot instead of a big-brand fried crisp, and try the bag. The work must not be framed as swapping people away from the loaves, which the client sees as a risk to avoid.
OBJECTIVE · ATTITUDINAL
To confirm: People believe a baked sourdough crisp can be properly crunchy. They feel it is a tasty, slightly special treat that is still an everyday choice, not a worthy compromise.
WHO WE ARE TALKING TO
To confirm: Core: office lunchers and students, mostly 18-35, grabbing a weekday meal deal and choosing the crisp in seconds, often on promotion. Many snack in place of a proper meal (62% of 16-34s). Secondary: parents buying for lunchboxes, and food-curious shoppers. Not for diet-first shoppers or loyal fried-crisp heavy users. The client said 18-55, so the narrower core is our proposal to confirm.
COMPETITOR CONTEXT
Walkers (about 26% volume share of potato chips) is the benchmark and leans on celebrity-led humour. Kettle Chips hold 'crafted' with thick-cut, hand-cooked crisps made in small batches, and Tyrrells sit in the same crafted space. Pringles own the familiar tube, and Propercorn (popcorn) claims 'better for you'. The open space is a baked crisp with real bakery credentials and audible crunch, without fleeing into health cues.
CONSUMER INSIGHT
The meal-deal crisp is the small treat that makes the lunch, and the crunch is the point. People want satisfaction without the greasy weight afterwards, but they won't trade taste for virtue: taste is the number one purchase driver.
SINGLE-MINDED PROPOSITION
Proper crunch, baked not fried.

THE OBJECTION TO BEAT
To confirm: "Baked crisps are dull and worthy, and not as good as a proper crisp."
REASONS TO BELIEVE
Made with Harrowgate's 80-year sourdough starter.
No palm oil.
40% less fat than the leading ready-salted fried crisps, lab tested, with the difference stated per the same quantity of food as the CAP Code requires.
To confirm: proof of the crunch itself, such as a texture test, a blind taste test against a leading fried crisp, or a recorded crunch measure. None has been supplied, so this is a gap to close before any crunch claim runs.
RESPONSE · THINK
To confirm: Baked sourdough crisps can be properly crunchy, and Harrowgate knows what it is doing.
RESPONSE · FEEL
To confirm: Delighted and a little cheeky, as if they have found a better snack that still feels like a treat.
RESPONSE · DO
To confirm: Pick up Harrowgate Oven Crisps in the meal-deal slot instead of a familiar fried bag. Sampling and sharing the crunch are routes to that pick-up.
TONE & WORLD
Warm but cheeky
Premium but everyday
Not like the big crisp brands
Sound-led and bakery-real
A treat, not health: no apologising
ROLE OF EACH MEDIUM
To confirm: Sampling: converts trial where the meal-deal choice is made.
Instagram and TikTok: carry the crunch sound for pre-Easter buzz.
Posters near stations and supermarkets: reach lunchers just before purchase.
15-second film: builds crunch credibility on YouTube and cinema.

HOW WE WILL KNOW IT WORKED
To confirm: Judged by: listings in 3 of the big 5 and 2 million bags in 12 weeks.
Attribution: sampled versus unsampled store comparison.
Before and after: To confirm: a tracker wave on belief in baked crunch.
In flight: meal-deal pick-up at sampled stores and sample-to-purchase.
The lag: buzz lands before Easter; repeat after.

MANDATORIES
The bag is the hero, logo top left.
No real, recognisable faces.
Never show the product with alcohol.
Include "Contains wheat. See pack for allergens."
No compostable claim anywhere at launch.
The fat claim must name its comparator and state the difference per the same quantity of food.
Do not show or mention bread or the pub.
OPEN QUESTIONS
What is the 40% less fat claim measured against (which fried crisp, which test), and is it substantiated? — Answer: It is measured against the leading ready-salted fried crisps, lab tested. Say 40% less fat than the leading fried crisps, and name the comparator on pack.
When is compostable bag certification expected, and can we mention it before then? — Answer: Certification is expected in May, after launch. Do not mention compostable anywhere in the launch work. Say 'recyclable where facilities exist' only if legal agrees; otherwise say nothing about the bag.
What are the sales, distribution and trial targets for launch and after Easter? — Answer: Launch: listed in 3 of the big 5 supermarket meal deals by March, 2 million bags sold in the first 12 weeks. After Easter: repeat purchase above 25 percent of triers.
What is the budget, and does it include VAT and media? — Answer: The budget is 180,000 pounds ex VAT, media included. Treat that as one national poster burst, social, one 15 second film and sampling in about 200 stores.
Which supermarkets have listed the crisps and are they confirmed in meal deals? — Answer: Two supermarkets have confirmed meal-deal listings for March; the third is in talks. Do not name them in the work.
Is swapping people away from your own sourdough loaves a goal, or a risk to avoid? — Answer: A risk to avoid. The crisps are for the meal-deal snack slot, not a reason to leave the loaves. Do not show or mention bread as the thing to swap from.
Is the pub a real near-term target, or a later extension? — Answer: A later extension. The pub is out of scope for launch: no pub scenes, no pub media.
What is the 40% fat comparator, and what is the evidence? — Answer: Same answer as the fat question: the leading ready-salted fried crisps, lab tested.
When will compostable certification land? — Answer: Certification lands in May. No compostable claim at launch.
Is cannibalising the loaves acceptable? — Answer: Not acceptable. Do not frame the work as swapping away from the loaves.
