TIDEWELL SPARKLING WATER · V1 · 23 NOV 2026
Tide's In
CLIENT
Tidewell Sparkling Water, launching Tidewell Sea Mineral Sparkling, a lime and cucumber sparkling water with a pinch of sea minerals in a slim 330 ml can.
PROJECT
Tide's In
BUDGET & SCOPE
£150,000 including VAT, with media buying outside it and paid by the client. Deliverables: posters near stations and festival sites, Instagram and TikTok, a 15-second film that also runs as YouTube pre-roll, sampling at running events, and possibly radio. Timing: April teaser, May launch, no Christmas phase, still range out. Ideas due next Wednesday.
THE JOB
To confirm: Move a young professional from ordering a beer or a sugary can to picking Tidewell at the after-work round, without feeling they have opted out of it.
WHERE WE ARE
Tidewell Sea Mineral Sparkling launches in May, with an April teaser, into an after-work round where sparkling water is led by San Pellegrino (26.4% consideration) and Highland Spring (23.8%). Not drinking is mainstream among the young: 20% of Gen Z adults in the UK do not drink alcohol. Pubs are also a customer; the still range and Christmas are out.
THE BARRIER
A soft drink at the round feels like the dull, second-best choice that needs explaining. Beer is the default, sugary drinks are a guilty treat, and another sparkling water gives no reason to switch. Young adults also worry about alcohol's emotional impact, yet still have to handle social drinking norms.
PAGE 2 OF 5
OBJECTIVE · COMMERCIAL
In the six months after the May launch: 250,000 cans sampled and 800 pub and bar listings. Retail sales are read through sales data; no sales figure has been given.
OBJECTIVE · BEHAVIOURAL
Young professionals choose Tidewell at the after-work round in place of the beer or the sugary drink, and try it through sampling. Sharing on social, whether a challenge or a filter, is secondary.
OBJECTIVE · ATTITUDINAL
Tidewell is seen as a cool, grown-up choice: the one you are happy to hold at a festival and the sensible one in the office fridge, never a consolation prize. The still range benefits by association.
WHO WE ARE TALKING TO
Lead with 25-40 urban young professionals who go out after work and are cutting back on alcohol or sugar: the sober curious and the moderators. Some are saving money where a round costs a small fortune. Festival-goers and runners are supporting audiences. Parents and 'everyone who drinks water' are not the lead.
COMPETITOR CONTEXT
San Pellegrino leads water consideration at 26.4%. Highland Spring sells purity and origin: 'Water as nature intended'. Perrier was advertised as the 'Champagne of mineral water'. Alcohol-free beer stays in a beer frame: Heineken 0.0 was seen as 'a bloke drink'. The open space is a mineral, coastal, adult fizz that is neither wellness-pious nor a beer substitute.
CONSUMER INSIGHT
To confirm: I want to stay in the round without being the one who has to explain why I'm drinking a soft drink.
SINGLE-MINDED PROPOSITION
To confirm: Tidewell is the sea-fresh fizz that is grown-up enough to be seen with at any round.
PAGE 3 OF 5
THE OBJECTION TO BEAT
To confirm: "It's just water with bubbles."
REASONS TO BELIEVE
A pinch of sea minerals in every can, the product's own point of difference from plain sparkling water.
Zero sugar and zero calories, cleared by legal; any 'no added sugars' wording must still meet the register's conditions of use, as the ASA's Trip ruling shows.
A slim 330 ml can that is the hero on every piece, small enough to hold comfortably in a round.
RESPONSE · THINK
To confirm: Tidewell is a proper adult drink with real character, not just water with bubbles.
RESPONSE · FEEL
To confirm: Fresh, confident and included, with no sense of missing out.
RESPONSE · DO
To confirm: Pick up a Tidewell at the next after-work round, or ask for it at the pub.
TONE & WORLD
Fresh but grown up
Fun but not silly
Coastal but not cliché: no shells, anchors or seaside puns
Wry and confident, never preachy or wellness-pious
Clearly not like the big brands
ROLE OF EACH MEDIUM
To confirm: Posters near stations: catch the after-work commute, can as hero.
Instagram and TikTok: carry the share among 25-40s.
15-second film: build launch fame and double as YouTube pre-roll.
Sampling at running events: first taste, to drive trial.

HOW WE WILL KNOW IT WORKED
To confirm: Judged by: 250,000 cans sampled and 800 pub and bar listings in six months; retail sales data.
Before and after: To confirm: a tracker wave before launch on 'proper drink or fancy water', repeated after the flight.
In flight: sampling redemptions, shares and listings won.
The lag: repeat purchase and the tracker read come after the flight.

MANDATORIES
The can is the hero, with the logo bottom left.
No real, recognisable faces.
Never show the product with alcohol in shot.
Include: 'Contains naturally occurring sea minerals. Enjoy as part of a balanced diet.'
Clear 'Cornish' with legal before use.
Keep health-type wording within the CAP Code and the GB register.
